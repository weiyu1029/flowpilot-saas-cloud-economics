# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Decision | Primary metrics | Dashboard view |
|---|---|---|---|
| VP Product | Invest, optimize, reprice, or retire features | Adoption, unit cost, cost growth, quadrant | Feature Economics |
| Finance / FinOps | Budget, forecast, cost allocation, unit economics | Cloud cost, variance, margin, savings | Executive + FinOps |
| Customer Success | Prioritize outreach and pricing review | Customer cost ratio, health, MRR at risk | Customer Profitability |
| Engineering / Cloud Ops | Reduce waste and improve reliability | Service cost, environment cost, incidents, anomalies | AWS FinOps & Reliability |
| Executive Team | Select highest-value interventions | MRR, gross margin, business impact | Executive + Optimization |
| Data / Analytics | Maintain trusted metrics | Data quality, grain, lineage, refresh | Data & Architecture |
