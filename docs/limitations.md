# Limitations and Ethical Use

1. All companies, people, subscriptions, usage, incidents, and costs are synthetic.
2. AWS-style cost rates are illustrative and are not current AWS prices or a substitute for Pricing Calculator, Cost Explorer, or CUR.
3. The gross-margin metric includes only allocated cloud infrastructure cost and omits labor, support, payment processing, third-party software, and other COGS.
4. Customer health and margin-risk tiers are explainable heuristics, not validated predictions.
5. Recommendation savings and revenue uplift are scenarios, not guarantees.
6. The deterministic Insights Copilot does not call an LLM; optional model integration requires grounded retrieval, evaluation, privacy, and access controls.
7. Production data would require governance, lineage, data contracts, residency, retention, and legal/security review.
8. Correlation in the dashboard is not causal proof.
